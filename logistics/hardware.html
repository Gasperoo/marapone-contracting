Do I need a GPU?

No. Everything runs on the CPU. A GPU only makes runs faster on large invoice batches and customs docs; it's never required.

Does it phone home?

No. There are no third-party API calls and no telemetry on your documents. It works fully offline and can run air-gapped.

Where does it install?

On your existing laptop or workstation, or on a server / VM in your own environment. You own the machine and the code.
