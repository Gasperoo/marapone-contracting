Inside the app

WHAT IT LOOKS LIKE RUNNING.

Real screens from a live build, recorded on an actual drawing set — not mock-ups. Every one of these runs on your own hardware, on your own drawings, with nothing leaving the network.

01 — Quantity takeoff

MEASURED OFF THE SHEETS.

Rooms, areas, doors, windows and fixtures pulled straight out of the drawing set and the schedules — with every line traceable back to the sheet it came from.

  • ▸Room-by-room areas with a live total
  • ▸Door, window and fixture counts off the schedules
  • ▸Metric and imperial handled automatically
  • ▸Exports as JSON straight into the AI Estimator
02 — Code & coordination audit

WHAT THE DRAWINGS CONTRADICT.

The full set checked against the 2024 Ontario Building Code and against itself — clause references included, so a finding can be taken to the consultant rather than argued about.

  • ▸OBC, AODA, fire and zoning checks with clause references
  • ▸Cross-discipline coordination conflicts
  • ▸Scope gaps between the specification and the drawings
  • ▸Severity-ranked so you read the expensive ones first
03 — Tender risk

THE CLAUSES THAT COST MONEY.

The bid documents read for the terms that carry real exposure — bonding, liquidated damages, fair wage, restricted hours, sole-source products — before you commit to a number.

  • ▸Onerous clause detection across the tender package
  • ▸Bonding and insurance requirements surfaced
  • ▸Liquidated damages and schedule exposure flagged
  • ▸Each finding quoted with its source excerpt
04 — Change orders & deficiencies

AFTER AWARD, IT KEEPS WORKING.

The same engine tracks revision-to-revision drift and builds the deficiency list, so the exposure you priced at tender is the exposure you can still see at closeout.

  • ▸Revision compare — what changed between issues
  • ▸Change-order risk tracked against the original scope
  • ▸Deficiency lists generated and cross-referenced
  • ▸RFI backlog checked against the current drawings
